Little Tusk demo pages

Fictional German websites for trying Tusk by hand in this browser. Tusk is the elephant in the corner of every page.

How to try a page
  1. Click Tusk. The chat opens. Settings at the top of the chat has the mode.
  2. Pick the mode shown on the card: Show me how (Tusk points and waits) or Look after me (Tusk offers on its own).
  3. Say what is written under Say, in your own words if you like. Tusk needs a key under Settings, then Family settings.

Popups and banners

Cookie banner

A weather site with a cookie banner. Tusk should choose "Nur notwendige" (only necessary), never "accept all".

Look after me
Say nothing: Tusk offers by itself. Answer Yes.

Popup that comes back

Closing works for a moment, then the box returns with an error. Tusk must say it is still there, not claim success.

Look after me
Answer Yes to Tusk's offer and watch the chat log.

Consent dialog that grabs the keyboard

Behaves like the cookie dialogs of big video sites: it takes focus and treats letter keys as shortcuts. Typing to Tusk must still work.

Show me how
Open the chat and type: hello kfm
