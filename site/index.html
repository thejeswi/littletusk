Little Tusk

Little Tusk is a friendly little elephant that lives in your browser. It helps you find your way on any website: it points to the right button, explains what things mean, and, if you ask, does the clicking for you. It always asks before anything that cannot be undone.

How to get it (for now)

  1. Download the extension from github.com/thejeswi/littletusk and build it (npm install, then npm run build).
  2. In Chrome open chrome://extensions and switch on "Developer mode".
  3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the dist folder.

Setting it up for someone else?

Make a setup link and send it by email. The link opens Little Tusk on their computer and asks them to confirm.
